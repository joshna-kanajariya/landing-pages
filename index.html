Environmental Impact Comparison

⚠️ Fast Fashion Impact

  • • 2,700L water per cotton t-shirt
  • • 35% of ocean microplastics from synthetic textiles
  • • 10% of global carbon emissions
  • • 92 million tons textile waste annually
  • • Toxic chemicals in waterways

✅ Sustainable Fashion Impact

  • • 70% less water with organic cotton
  • • Biodegradable natural fibers
  • • Carbon-negative manufacturing
  • • Zero-waste production methods
  • • Non-toxic, eco-friendly dyes
